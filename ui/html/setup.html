Set up MetaForge Studio

Step 1 of 3

Your music library

MetaForge works on the music folders on this PC. Choose the top folder of your music library, and MetaForge will look at how it's organised.

For example F:\ or D:\Music. You can type it or choose it.

How your library is organised

MetaForge looked at your folders and chose the closest match. Change it if it's wrong; you can also change it later in Settings.

Category folders

These are the folders at the top of your library. Untick any that aren't music categories (a downloads folder, for example).

    Your email and service keys

    MusicBrainz asks for it with each lookup, so they can contact you about heavy use. It is sent only to MusicBrainz.

    Service keys

    All three are needed: Intelli-Tagger, MetaForge's main tagging tool, uses them to tag your music. They're free. MetaForge checks each one with its service before saving, and keeps them only on this PC.

    Used to work out genre, mood and more, and to write artist biographies. Get a key at Google AI Studio (opens in your browser)

    Used for release years, musician credits, artist photos and missing cover art. Get a token at Discogs Developers (opens in your browser)

    Used to recognise songs by their sound. Register an application to get one. Get a key at acoustid.org (opens in your browser)

    Your settings are saved. You can change any of them later in Settings.
